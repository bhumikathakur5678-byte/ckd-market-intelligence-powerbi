## About project

Global CKD Market Intelligence

### Objectives

1. Estimate the global CKD market size (2025 to 2034)
2. Analyze historical and forecast market growth using CAGR
3. Identify major therapy segments and growth potential
4. Evaluate regional market distribution and competitive landscape
5. Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for market intelligence and business decision-making

### Methodology

1. **Secondary Research.** Reviewed industry reports, journals, company annual reports and public databases.
2. **WHO / GBD / KDIGO Data Collection.** Collected epidemiology & prevalence data from WHO, GBD & KDIGO guidelines.
3. **Company Annual Reports & SEC Filings.** Gathered financials, product portfolios and pipeline information of key players.
4. **Data Cleaning & Validation (Excel).** Standardized, cleaned and validated data for accuracy and consistency.
5. **Market Sizing & Data Triangulation.** Top-down & bottom-up approaches with triangulation for final market estimates.
6. **Segmentation & Forecast Analysis.** Segmented by drug class, treatment type, distribution channel & region. Forecasted market (2026 to 2034) using CAGR.
7. **Power BI Dashboard Development.** Built interactive dashboard for KPIs, trends, segmentation and competitive intelligence.
8. **Result Validation & Market Intelligence.** Validated results with multiple sources and translated into actionable insights.

## SWOT analysis

**Strengths:** Strong clinical adoption of established therapies; Robust pipeline across key companies; Growing awareness and early diagnosis.

**Weaknesses:** Late diagnosis and low awareness in many regions; High treatment cost; Limited access & dependence on chronic therapy adherence.

**Opportunities:** Rising CKD prevalence and aging population; High growth potential in emerging markets; Expansion of SGLT2 inhibitors and novel therapies.

**Threats:** Patent expiries & biosimilar drugs; Pricing pressure and reimbursement challenges; Regulatory changes and market access hurdles.

## Challenges

- Incomplete or inconsistent market data across sources
- Wide variation in market-size estimates
- Limited access to paid databases & primary data
- Reconciling epidemiology vs revenue data
- Time constraints for deep country-level analysis

## Conclusion: answering the objectives

- **Market Size Estimation:** The global CKD market is estimated at USD 30.9 Bn in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 67.8 Bn by 2034.
- **Growth Forecast (CAGR):** The market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 9.2% during 2026 to 2034, showing a strong upward trend.
- **Therapy Segments:** ACE inhibitors lead the market (26.5%), while SGLT2 inhibitors are the fastest-growing drug class with high future potential.
- **Regional Distribution:** North America holds the largest share (38%), followed by Europe (28%) and Asia Pacific (27%), with Asia Pacific expected to grow the fastest.
- **Market Drivers & Landscape:** Rising CKD prevalence, aging population, and innovation in therapies are key drivers shaping the competitive landscape.
- **Dashboard Development:** An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to track KPIs, market sizing, segmentation, forecasts, and competitor analysis for data-driven decision-making.
- **Business Opportunity:** High unmet need, early diagnosis adoption, and expansion in emerging markets present significant growth opportunities.
- **Overall Impact:** The project provided comprehensive insights into the global CKD market and strengthened skills in research, analytics and dashboard development to support strategic decisions.
